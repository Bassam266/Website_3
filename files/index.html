About

Physicist working across imaging, sensing & materials

Bassam Jameel is a physicist and postdoctoral researcher specialising in ultrasound systems, acoustic sensing, and photoacoustic imaging. His doctoral research focused on the quantitative, non-destructive characterisation of complex colloidal systems — including magnetic fluids and Pickering emulsions — using ultrasound attenuation spectroscopy, acoustic scattering modelling, and numerical simulation.

He is currently a postdoctoral researcher at the Fresnel Institute (Aix-Marseille University / CNRS), developing high-frequency optical ultrasound sensors for photoacoustic imaging. His work spans sensor fabrication, signal-chain optimisation, acoustic field modelling, and image reconstruction — bridging biomedical imaging, non-destructive evaluation, and materials characterisation.
