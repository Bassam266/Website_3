About

Physicist working across sensing, imaging & modelling

I work on high-frequency optical ultrasound sensors for photoacoustic imaging, and on modelling how ultrasound scatters through complex matter — magnetic fluids, Pickering emulsions, and other soft, structured media. My work spans device-level sensor development, signal processing, and image reconstruction, connecting physical measurements to quantitative models.
